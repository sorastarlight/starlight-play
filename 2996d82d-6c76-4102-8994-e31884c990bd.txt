The **GS Ball** (Japanese: **ＧＳボール** *GS Ball*) is a [Key Item](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Key_Item) exclusive to [Pokémon Crystal](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Crystal_Version). It is a special and mysterious [Poké Ball](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9_Ball) whose design appears to be a reference to [Pokémon Gold and Silver](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Gold_and_Silver_Versions), the first [core series](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Core_series) games of [Generation II](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Generation_II), as the ball has the letters "GS" inscribed upon it and is colored both gold and silver.
An [NPC](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Non-player_character) in [Pokémon Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Ultra_Sun_and_Ultra_Moon) [mentions](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_cross-generational_references) the GS Ball, claiming that it stands for **greatest smith's ball** (Japanese: **ガンテツすごいボール** *Gantetsu's Superb Ball*) or **Gold-Silver Ball** (Japanese: **ゴールドシルバーボール** *Gold-Silver Ball*).

### Effect
In [Pokémon Crystal](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Crystal_Version), the player can show the GS Ball to [Kurt](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kurt) at his home in [Azalea Town](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Azalea_Town) and later place this item in the [Ilex Forest shrine](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ilex_Forest_shrine). This results in summoning a [level](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Level) 30 [wild](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Wild_Pok%C3%A9mon) [Celebi](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Celebi_(Pok%C3%A9mon)), consuming the GS Ball.
Although the GS Ball can only be legitimately obtained after the player has entered the [Hall of Fame](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hall_of_Fame), this event is technically able to work normally as soon as [Team Rocket](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Team_Rocket) has been defeated in the [Slowpoke Well](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Slowpoke_Well) and [Kurt](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kurt) has returned to his home. This event still works even if the charcoal maker's son and his [Farfetch'd](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Farfetch'd_(Pok%C3%A9mon)) are still in [Ilex Forest](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ilex_Forest) (such as if the player has a Pokémon with [Cut](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Cut_(move)) from a trade instead of obtaining [HM01](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/HM01)).
[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/0/04/Kurt_-_restless_forest.png)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/File:Kurt_-_restless_forest.png)Kurt noting that [Ilex Forest](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ilex_Forest) is restless during the GS Ball event
Kurt borrows this item to analyze it, if the player talks with him while the GS Ball is in the [Bag](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Bag) and an internal event flag indicates that it has been legitimately obtained, as long as he is not busy making any Poké Balls from [Apricorns](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Apricorn) and does not have any ready to deliver. Kurt will not offer to make any Poké Balls for the duration of this event. When the player talks with him again after a day has passed, he notes that this item has started to shake and that Ilex Forest is restless, then leaves the house. After leaving as well, the player automatically follows Kurt and gets the GS Ball back, now being able to summon Celebi by interacting with the shrine. This requires a Pokémon with [Cut](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Cut_(move)) and the [Hive Badge](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Badge#Hive_Badge) since the player must go through a [cuttable tree](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Cuttable_tree).
If the player [blacks out](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Black_out) from [poison](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Poison_(status_condition)) inside Kurt's house instead of leaving normally after Kurt has left, they can still talk to Kurt in the Azalea Town outdoors and get the GS Ball back. However, if the player has another GS Ball at this point (such as one obtained from the [Celebi Egg glitch](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Celebi_Egg_glitch)), it is now possible to summon Celebi without talking to Kurt.
As long as the player is able to summon Celebi, strong winds blow over Ilex Forest, as seen by the tree animations. Instead of delivering [TM12](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/TM12) ([Sweet Scent](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sweet_Scent_(move))), the [Teacher](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Teacher_(Trainer_class)) in Ilex Forest's north [gate](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Gate) (from [Route 34](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Johto_Route_34)) blocks the way, so the player has no choice but entering through the south gate (from Azalea Town). The [Lass](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Lass_(Trainer_class)) who was originally in the north gate appears inside the forest; she blocks the way so the player is unable to go far past the shrine. Since the Teacher and the Lass are blocking the player's way, it will not be possible to obtain the [Antidote](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Antidote), [Ether](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ether), hidden [Full Heal](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Full_Heal), [TM02](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/TM02) ([Headbutt](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Headbutt_(move))), and [X Attack](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/X_Attack), or battle [Bug Catcher](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Bug_Catcher_(Trainer_class)) Wayne inside the forest, until the player battles Celebi.
The player will have a single chance to battle Celebi. Its [individual values](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Individual_values) are randomly generated when the battle starts, with a chance of it being [Shiny](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Shiny_Pok%C3%A9mon). If the player defeats it or runs away from the [battle](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_battle), it will be [permanently unavailable](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_permanently_missable_Pok%C3%A9mon) in the current [saved](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Save) game. The Teacher and Lass return to their original places after the battle. If Celebi was caught, Kurt appears in the forest to talk with the player.

### Distribution
In the original Japanese release of [Pokémon Crystal](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Crystal_Version), [players](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Player_character) could obtain a GS Ball as an [event item](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Event_item) from the [Trade Corner attendant](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Center_lady#Sprites) through the [Mobile System GB](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mobile_System_GB) and the Pokémon News Machine at the Pokémon Communication Center in Goldenrod City.
For the original Western localizations, the distribution event was adapted to the Goldenrod Pokémon Center but was never used.
The [Celebi Egg glitch](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Celebi_Egg_glitch) can be used to obtain Celebi directly from an [Egg](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Egg). It is also possible to use this glitch to obtain the GS Ball as a [held item](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Held_item), but this does not trigger the Celebi event.
In the [Virtual Console](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Virtual_Console) releases, the GS Ball no longer requires an event distribution as in the original games. It can be obtained after entering the [Hall of Fame](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hall_of_Fame) upon exiting the Pokémon Communication Center in the Japanese version or the Goldenrod Pokémon Center in the Western localizations.

## In animation
### *Pokémon the Series*
[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/4/42/GS_Ball_anime.png/250px-GS_Ball_anime.png)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/File:GS_Ball_anime.png)The GS Ball in [*Pokémon the Series: The Beginning*](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Original_series)
#### [*Pokémon the Series: The Beginning*](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Original_series)
In the [animated series](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_animated_series), [Professor Ivy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Professor_Ivy) was the first character to possess the GS Ball. She called upon the help of [Professor Oak](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Professor_Oak_(anime)) to study it, but couldn't figure out how to open it. Ivy tried to open it with hacksaws, hammers, crowbars, power drills and lasers, but to no avail. At one point, Ash tried simply calling a Pokémon to be released, but this did not work. The ball also couldn't be transmitted via [PC](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PC).
In *[Pallet Party Panic](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/EP081)*, Professor Oak tasked [Ash](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ash_Ketchum) with retrieving the GS Ball from Professor Ivy's laboratory, located on [Valencia Island](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Valencia_Island) in the [Orange Islands](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Orange_Islands), and bringing it back to Oak for him to study.
Ash obtained the Ball two episodes later, in *[Poké Ball Peril](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/EP083)*, and updated Oak on his progress. He put off completing the errand in the [next episode](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/EP084) so he could compete in the [Orange League](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Orange_League), a move which Oak approved.
In *[Fit To Be Tide](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/EP085)*, [Misty](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Misty_(anime)) scolded her [Psyduck](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Misty's_Psyduck) for bouncing the Ball up and down on its head.
In *[A Way Off Day Off](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/EP098)*, Ash compared the GS Ball to a standard [Poké Ball](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9_Ball_(item)) while everyone else was sleeping.
After a brief mention by Misty in *[The Underground Round Up](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/EP114)*, Ash finally gave Oak the GS Ball after returning to [Pallet Town](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pallet_Town) in *[A Tent Situation](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/EP115)*, but Oak put it away to examine later. The GS Ball was then last sucked in by [Team Rocket](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Team_Rocket_trio)'s [Super Sucker](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Team_Rocket's_mechas). When the trio's balloon was torn, the GS Ball was rescued with all the other Poké Balls.
In *[The Rivalry Revival](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/EP116)*, Oak's studies on the ball proved to be extremely difficult, with [Tracey](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Tracey_Sketchit) even admitting that no new information was gathered. Oak gave it back to Ash, asking him to deliver the GS Ball to Poké Ball researcher and manufacturer [Kurt](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kurt) of [Azalea Town](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Azalea_Town) in [Johto](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Johto).
#### [*Pokémon the Series: Gold and Silver*](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Original_series)
In *[Once in a Blue Moon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/EP127)*, Ash took the Ball out to polish it when it is stolen by a [Quagsire](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Quagsire_(Pok%C3%A9mon)). Ash, Misty, and [Brock](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Brock_(anime)) chased after the Quagsire before getting the Ball back by the end of the episode.
In *[A Shadow of a Drought](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/EP142)*, [the group](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ash's_friends) arrived in Azalea Town, and Misty reminded Ash about the errand. The delivery was made in *[Going Apricorn!](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/EP143)*, and Ash broke the news to Oak in *[A Farfetch'd Tale](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/EP145)*. Its last appearance was in a flashback in *[No Big Woop!](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/EP148)*.
In an [interview](http://www.pokebeach.com/news/0708/second-pokemon-interview-with-masamitsu-hidaka-many-interesting-points) with [Masamitsu Hidaka](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Masamitsu_Hidaka) in 2008, it was explained that the GS Ball was originally going to contain a [Celebi](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Celebi_(Pok%C3%A9mon)) that was to be the star of a large arc of the Johto saga. However, it was decided that Celebi would be the star of [the fourth movie](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/M04), so the story arc was viewed as redundant and shelved. The Ball was left with Kurt with the hopes that viewers would eventually forget about it. This concept would later be recycled with [Meloetta](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Meloetta_(anime)) during [*Pokémon the Series: Black & White*](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_the_Series:_Black_&_White).
For every episode of [Pokémon: Johto League Champions](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/S04), three GS Balls are seen alongside the [title card](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Title_card) and the name of the episode. Each Ball is situated on a corner of the title card; there are two GS Balls on the two bottom corners and one on the top right corner.

### Pokémon Adventures
#### [*Gold, Silver & Crystal* arc](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Gold,_Silver_&_Crystal_arc_(Adventures))
The GS Ball was created by the [Masked Man](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Masked_Man) in *[The Last Battle XII](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS178)* using a net made from [the](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Silver_Feather) [feathers](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rainbow_Feather) of [Lugia and Ho-Oh](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Tower_duo_(Adventures)), after stealing its recipe from [Kurt](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kurt) in *[The Last Battle I](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS167)*. He used it to capture [Celebi](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Celebi_(Adventures)) at the [Ilex Forest shrine](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ilex_Forest_shrine) in *[The Last Battle XIV](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/PS180)*. Only the feathers of the two [Legendary Pokémon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Legendary_Pok%C3%A9mon) could contain Celebi and prevent their holder from suffering the ill effects of [time travel](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Time_travel). [Gold](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Gold_(Adventures)) was able to use his [Pichu](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pichu_(Pok%C3%A9mon)), [Pibu](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pibu), to destroy the ball and set Celebi free.
## Trivia
[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/e/e4/Labeled_Teru-Sama_GoldSilver-KOR.png)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/File:Labeled_Teru-Sama_GoldSilver-KOR.png)A "GS Ball" is one of three dummy items in Korean Gold and Silver
* In the Korean versions of [Pokémon Gold and Silver](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Gold_and_Silver_Versions), the dummy item [Teru-sama](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Teru-sama) with ID 0x73 is labeled "GS Ball" (Korean: **GS볼** *GS Ball*). This item ID is the same one used by the GS Ball in Crystal and [Pokémon Stadium 2](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Stadium_2), and is called Teru-sama in Japanese and Western Gold and Silver.
* Despite being recognized as a Poké Ball, in [Pokémon Crystal](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Crystal_Version), the GS Ball cannot contain any Pokémon, instead serving as a way to summon [Celebi](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Celebi_(Pok%C3%A9mon)) at the [Ilex Forest shrine](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ilex_Forest_shrine).
