 PS Custom Avatars All new custom avatars that are uploaded to PS will be logged in this thread, along with credit to the artists who made them. Big thanks to everyone on the Quality Control team for reviewing the sprites here as well as to Marty for uploading each sprite! Current QC Team: Altthiel Brumirage Clem deetah emillight Flamibane Grapo HSBT Irpachuza Kalalokki kyledove RADU sharpclaw Turtleye_ Check the Custom Avatar Quality Guidelines if you are going to sprite or commission a custom avatar. You can also check out the PS! Custom Avatars Index. This database has the main objective of helping us track down as many artist credits as possible. So if you have a custom avatar (or have worked on any) and you don't see it there or it lacks proper credits, please DM Irpachuza or deetah at Discord (same usernames) and they'll add them promptly. Every new credit is a step towards recognizing our artists properly, so any info is appreciated!​
