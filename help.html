How to Play

ST★RLIGHT Pokémon RPG — play alongside Sora's livestream.

  1. Join a wild encounter when a Pokémon appears during the stream.
  2. Prepare — choose a Berry, Honey, or No Item.
  3. Throw — choose a Poké Ball.
  4. See whether you caught it. Catches aren't guaranteed.
  5. Build your Pokédex, PC, and Trainer ID as you play.

More details

Open a topic only if you need it. You do not need to learn everything before your first catch.

Poké Balls

Choose a Poké Ball during Throw. Poké Ball is the everyday choice. Great Ball and Ultra Ball are stronger and cost more. Specialist Balls shine under certain conditions — cards explain when.

Berries

Berries are personal. They help only your catch attempt. Open a Berry card during Prepare to see its effect.

Honey

Honey is a community contribution. More Trainers contributing Honey helps the shared encounter. It does not replace your Poké Ball and does not guarantee a catch.

PokéCoins & Mart

PokéCoins are earned from play and spent at Starlight Mart for Balls, Berries, Honey, and other supplies.

Evolution & Evolution Lines

Catch Pokémon and earn Evolution Candy along an Evolution Line. When a Pokémon is ready, open Evolution to evolve it.

Species Mastery

Duplicate catches still matter. Catching the same species builds Species Mastery.

Trainer Level & Trainer ID

Playing earns XP and unlocks cosmetics. Your Trainer ID grows with badges, titles, and showcase picks — customize when you are ready.

Special Events

Special Events are scheduled opportunities for rare Pokémon. Check Events for what, when, and play them on Play when live.

Starlight Pass

Optional subscriber support with daily and weekly claims. Ordinary progression does not require the Pass.

Twitch Power-Ups

Optional stream support with guaranteed in-game supplies. Never required to catch Pokémon. No paid Pokémon, Shinies, or Legendary access.

Twitch linking

Connect Twitch so the RPG knows which Trainer is participating in chat and on stream. You can still browse public Pokédex info, Rankings, Events, and Trainer profiles without it.
