PROFESSOR OAK'S EVOLUTION RESEARCH

Catch duplicate Pokémon from the same Evolution Line and send them to Professor Oak to earn Evolution Candy.

Use Evolution Candy to evolve a Pokémon you already own. Evolution transforms that Pokémon into its evolved form—it does not create an additional copy.

Some Pokémon also require special evolution items. Evolution Stones and Linking Cords can be purchased from the Starlight Mart.

Only Pokémon currently available in the game can be evolved into. While Kanto is the active region, evolutions or relatives introduced in later generations will remain locked until their corresponding content is released.
