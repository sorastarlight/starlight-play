Back Sprites:poketool / trga / trbgraFront Sprites:poketool / trga / trfgraHM Summon Sprites:data / field_cutinVS Faces:graphic / field_encountereffectIMPORTANT: For some reason the “mugshots” in platinum do not use the palettes in the field_encountereffect narc. Strangely, they actually pull fromthe palette in the front sprite narc above for that same character. So you need to take some extra steps to make the narc in field_encountereffect perfectly match the one in trfgra. You can do this by exporting the front sprite palette and importing it over the palette mugshot narc, or editing the mugshot narc with console tool. You can also see another work around demonstrated in this video:
